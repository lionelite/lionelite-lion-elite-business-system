# Clinic purchasing funnel | Pilot v1
Use the same stages for all ten accounts in issue #15. Keep buyer details, private quotes and activity in the CRM. Assign one owner and one dated next action per open account.

| Stage | Rep talking point or action | Evidence to advance |
|---|---|---|
| Identify buyer | “Who reviews supplier pricing for your clinic?” | Buyer or authorized reviewer confirms role |
| Confirm fit | “Which item would be worth comparing, at what order size, and what requirements must a supplier meet?” | Buyer names item, quantity, requirements and buying window |
| Can we do it? | Verify sourcing, intended use, availability and documentation for that exact request. Tell the buyer which requirements are confirmed and when open questions will be answered. | Documented fit and buyer permission to quote. Stop if the product is unsuitable for the intended use |
| How does it work? | “Who reviews the documentation, who approves the order, and when do you need delivery?” Confirm fulfillment and payment terms before promising them. | Approval path and review date agreed |
| What does it cost? | Quote the same specification and quantity, showing unit price, shipping, fees, total, and validity date. “If these terms work, what needs to happen to place the first order?” | Written quote delivered to buyer with decision date |
| Close and collect | Confirm acceptance, issue invoice under agreed terms, reconcile funds. | Separate accepted, invoiced and paid timestamps. Count revenue collected only on confirmed payment |
| Fulfill and reorder | Confirm receipt and actual costs. “When should we review the next order?” | Actual contribution recorded and reorder date or close reason logged |

## Route the response
| Buyer response | Next move |
|---|---|
| Wrong person | Ask for the purchasing contact or a transfer. Remain in discovery |
| Send information | Ask which requirement matters most, send the relevant document and agree on a review date |
| Already have a supplier | Ask whether a backup quote on one item would be useful. Continue only with interest |
| Too expensive | Compare delivered cost at equivalent quantity and specification. Requote only if economics support it |
| Need approval | Ask who signs and offer a joint quote review |
| Later | Ask for the buying window and permission to follow up on a specific date |
| No interest or opt-out | Close with reason. Suppress future outreach for opt-outs |
| No response | Log the attempt separately from objections. Check prior activity before any follow-up |

## Pilot scorecard
Record account ID, rep, stage, date, buyer's words, response category, next action and due date. For quotes add quote ID, amount, decision date, paid amount and actual costs. Keep one account per purchasing authority.
Review after ten distinct accounts. Report counts and denominators for buyers reached per account attempted, quotes per buyer reached, and paid accounts per quoted account. Separate open quotes from losses. Report cash collected and contribution after refunds, product, shipping, payment fees, commissions and attributable outreach costs. Deduct pilot outreach costs even for accounts that never buy. Track paid reorders separately.
Compare repeated objections by stage. Change one talking point for the next cohort and retain the prior version for comparison.
