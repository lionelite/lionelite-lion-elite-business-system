# Lion Elite Developer Delivery OS

## Purpose

Lion Elite sells measurable business outcomes and manages delivery through vetted specialists. The client relationship, solution design, quality control, intellectual property, and final acceptance remain under Lion Elite control.

## Non-negotiable financial gates

- Collect 50–70% before assigning development.
- Target at least 60% forecast gross margin at signing.
- Do not let total fulfillment cost exceed 40% of contract value without owner approval.
- Release developer payments by accepted milestone, not by elapsed time.
- Keep 10% of the developer fee until final client acceptance and handoff.
- Quote change requests separately before work begins.

## Project flow

1. **Qualify:** Confirm the problem is expensive, measurable, and owned by a decision-maker.
2. **Scope:** Define the outcome, exclusions, integrations, access requirements, milestones, and acceptance tests.
3. **Price:** Price the client on value. Estimate fulfillment cost separately and check the margin in Delivery Control.
4. **Collect:** Receive the agreed deposit before scheduling implementation.
5. **Assign:** Select a developer by proven specialty and availability. Share only the approved scope.
6. **Build:** Require work on a Lion Elite-controlled repository and milestone branch.
7. **Verify:** Run security, functionality, error-handling, mobile, and acceptance checks before showing the client.
8. **Accept:** Record milestone acceptance before releasing its payment.
9. **Launch:** Deploy from company-controlled accounts, deliver documentation, and train the client.
10. **Retain:** Convert support, optimization, reporting, and additional automations into a monthly retainer.

## Required statement of work

Every project must define:

- business outcome and success metric;
- exact deliverables and explicit exclusions;
- client dependencies and access deadlines;
- milestone dates, developer payout, and objective acceptance test;
- revision limit and change-order rate;
- ownership and IP transfer terms;
- confidentiality and data-handling requirements;
- warranty window and ongoing-support boundary.

## Developer controls

- Signed contractor agreement, confidentiality, IP assignment, and non-solicitation before access.
- Least-privilege access; never share owner credentials or production secrets in tickets.
- Company-controlled GitHub repository, cloud account, domains, analytics, and billing.
- Pull request review required before merge.
- No direct pricing or commercial negotiation with the client.
- No production deployment without owner approval and a rollback plan.
- Credentials removed immediately at project completion or contractor termination.

## Core offer

**AI Revenue Recovery System:** capture missed leads, respond rapidly, qualify prospects, follow up automatically, schedule appointments, and report attributable pipeline and revenue.

Sell the outcome—not hours, prompts, or a generic chatbot. Begin with one workflow that can produce or protect more value than the project fee.

## Weekly owner review

- Cash collected versus contracted revenue
- Forecast profit and gross margin by project
- Projects below 50% margin
- Overdue projects and milestones
- Work awaiting acceptance
- Scope changes not yet quoted
- Contractor quality, timeliness, and rework
- Retainer opportunities after launch
