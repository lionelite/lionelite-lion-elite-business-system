# Entity Structure Map

## Target architecture

```text
Irrevocable Trust
      |
      v
Holding / Parent LLC
      |
      +-- Operating Company A
      +-- Operating Company B
      +-- Digital / IP Company
      +-- Real Estate LLC 1
      +-- Real Estate LLC 2
      +-- Investment LLC (if appropriate)
```

## Entity register

| Entity | Owner | Activity | Tax classification | Payroll? | Holds appreciating assets? | Liability risk | CPA confirmed? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Parent/Holding LLC | Trust | Ownership/management | TBD | TBD | Yes | Low/Medium | No |
| Operating LLC | Parent | Active business | TBD | TBD | Prefer limited | High | No |
| IP LLC | Parent/Trust | Trademarks/digital assets | TBD | Usually no | Yes | Low | No |
| Property LLC | Parent/Trust | Real estate | TBD | Usually no | Yes | Medium | No |

## Design rules

- Keep operating liabilities away from valuable long-term assets where practical.
- Do not elect S-corp taxation until trust eligibility to own S-corp stock is confirmed.
- Do not transfer mortgaged property without checking lender, due-on-sale, insurance, title, and tax consequences.
- Document every transfer with ownership records, assignments, valuations, and basis information.
- Keep separate bank accounts and books for each entity.
