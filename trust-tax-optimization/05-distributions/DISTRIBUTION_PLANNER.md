## Decision process

1. Confirm the trustee has authority to make the distribution.
2. Determine whether the payment is income, principal, or another type of distribution under the trust and tax rules.
3. Estimate DNI and tax character before distributing.
4. Compare trust-level tax with beneficiary-level tax.
5. Consider state taxes for both trust and beneficiary.
6. Check whether capital gains remain taxed to the trust or can be included in DNI under the governing document and applicable law.
7. Record trustee approval and distribution purpose.
8. Issue and reconcile K-1 information after year-end.

Do not make distributions solely for tax reasons if they conflict with the trust terms or fiduciary duties.
