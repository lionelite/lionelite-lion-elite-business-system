# Compliance Calendar

## Monthly
- Reconcile trust and entity bank accounts.
- Keep trust, business, and personal spending separate.
- Save invoices, contracts, receipts, distributions, contributions, and transfer records.
- Record related-party transactions with purpose and supporting agreement.

## Quarterly
- Update estimated trust/entity taxable income.
- Review estimated-tax payments.
- Review payroll and reasonable-compensation compliance for any S corporation.
- Update asset values for major changes.

## Annual
- Confirm trust tax classification.
- Prepare Form 1041 if required.
- Prepare beneficiary K-1s if required.
- Prepare entity returns and information returns.
- Reconcile distributions and trustee records.
- Review depreciation schedules.
- Review ownership/cap tables and operating agreements.
- Review insurance/title alignment.
- Run year-end retain-vs-distribute tax analysis for non-grantor trust income.
- Review new appreciating assets for possible planning.
- Review upcoming sales before they become binding.

## Document retention
Maintain copies of signed trust amendments, tax returns, K-1s, valuations, transfer documents, deeds, assignments, entity agreements, minutes/consents, loan documents, distribution records, and professional approvals.
