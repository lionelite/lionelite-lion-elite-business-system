## Before transferring an appreciating asset

1. Confirm the trust is permitted to own it.
2. Determine whether the transfer is a gift, sale, contribution, assignment, or other transaction.
3. Record tax basis and acquisition date.
4. Obtain a defensible valuation where required or advisable.
5. Determine gift-tax/reporting consequences.
6. Check debt, lender, title, insurance, securities, operating-agreement, and transfer restrictions.
7. Confirm income-tax and state-tax consequences.
8. Execute the transfer documents.
9. Update company books, cap table, title, contracts, and insurance.
10. Save CPA/attorney approval and all supporting records.
