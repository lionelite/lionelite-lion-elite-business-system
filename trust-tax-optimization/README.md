# Trust Tax Optimization Framework

Purpose: organize the trust, entities, assets, tax elections, distributions, records, and professional review needed to maximize lawful tax advantages while keeping liability and compliance clean.

## Core structure

Irrevocable Trust
→ Holding / Parent LLC
→ Separate operating LLCs
→ Separate real-estate LLCs
→ Separate IP / investment entities where appropriate

## Main goals

1. Move high-growth assets into the right ownership structure before major appreciation.
2. Confirm whether the trust is grantor or non-grantor and model taxes accordingly.
3. Avoid unnecessary income accumulation inside a high-tax non-grantor trust.
4. Use beneficiary distributions only when authorized and tax-efficient.
5. Select tax treatment independently for each subsidiary.
6. Separate operating risk from valuable assets and intellectual property.
7. Preserve documentation for basis, valuations, transfers, distributions, loans, and deductions.
8. Plan before major asset or business sales.

## Rule

No transfer, tax election, distribution, related-party payment, sale, loan, or ownership change should be implemented from this repository alone. Each major action should be cleared by the trust attorney and CPA first.

- `05-distributions/` — beneficiary distribution planning and records
- `06-real-estate/` — property LLCs, depreciation, financing, 1031 planning
