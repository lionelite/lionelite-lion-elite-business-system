# IP Holding Framework

Use this to separate valuable intellectual property from operating risk when appropriate.

## Assets to track
- Trademarks
- Brand names
- Domains
- Copyrightable content
- Software/code
- Courses/digital products
- Proprietary systems/processes
- Licensing rights

## Planning questions
- Who owns the IP today?
- Should the trust, parent LLC, or a separate IP LLC own it?
- Will operating companies license the IP?
- Is a written license agreement needed?
- Are royalties commercially reasonable and documented?
- Does moving the IP create gift, income, state, or valuation consequences?
- Is a third-party valuation needed?

## Rule

Do not create artificial royalty deductions or related-party charges solely to shift taxable income. Any licensing structure must reflect actual ownership, real business purpose, proper documentation, and CPA/attorney approval.
