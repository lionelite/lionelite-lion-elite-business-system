# Pre-Sale / Liquidity Event Checklist

Run this before signing a binding sale agreement whenever a business, property, IP asset, or highly appreciated investment may be sold.

## Before the deal is locked
- Identify the exact seller/taxpayer.
- Pull tax basis and holding period.
- Estimate ordinary income, capital gain, depreciation recapture, and state tax.
- Review whether the asset should remain where it is or whether a lawful pre-sale transfer is appropriate.
- Review installment-sale treatment where relevant.
- Review 1031 eligibility for qualifying real estate.
- Review charitable planning for highly appreciated assets before sale.
- Review estate/gift implications of transferring appreciation before a transaction becomes effectively certain.
- Obtain valuation if ownership interests may be transferred.
- Confirm no step-transaction, assignment-of-income, or substance-over-form issue is being created.
