# CPA + Trust Attorney Checklist

Use this as the agenda for professional review.

## Trust attorney
- Confirm grantor/non-grantor status and exact tax powers.
- Confirm trustee authority for business ownership, distributions, investments, loans, and asset sales.
- Confirm whether trust can own S-corp stock and whether QSST/ESBT treatment is needed.
- Confirm beneficiary and distribution provisions.
- Review asset-protection and estate-inclusion consequences.
- Review proposed transfers before execution.
- Confirm state governing-law and trust-residency issues.

## CPA
- Model grantor vs non-grantor income-tax treatment.
- Model retain-vs-distribute strategy annually.
- Review each entity’s tax classification.
- Model S-corp savings where eligible.
- Review basis, depreciation, passive losses, and 1031 opportunities.
- Review state taxation of trust, entities, grantor, and beneficiaries.
- Confirm estimated-tax and filing requirements.
- Review related-party transactions and documentation.
- Build a pre-sale tax model before major transactions.
