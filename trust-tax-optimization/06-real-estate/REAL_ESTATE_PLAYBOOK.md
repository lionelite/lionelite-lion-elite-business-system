# Real Estate Playbook

## Structure

Prefer one property or liability pool per LLC when practical. Keep title, insurance, financing, accounting, and ownership aligned.

## Tax opportunities to review
- Depreciation on qualifying rental/investment property.
- Cost segregation where the economics justify it.
- Deductible operating expenses and interest subject to applicable limitations.
- Suspended passive losses and when they can be used.
- 1031 exchange planning before a qualifying sale closes.
- Installment-sale treatment where appropriate.
- Entity and state tax treatment for each property.

## Before acquiring
- Decide purchaser/entity before closing.
- Confirm trust/LLC ownership is compatible with financing.
- Review personal guarantees and liability exposure.
- Set bookkeeping and bank account.
- Capture closing statement and initial basis allocation.

## Before selling
- Pull adjusted basis and depreciation schedule.
- Estimate depreciation recapture and capital gain.
- Decide whether 1031, installment, charitable, or direct sale planning is relevant before signing/closing.
- Confirm the selling taxpayer/entity is the same taxpayer that must complete any intended deferral strategy.
- Save CPA and attorney approval.
