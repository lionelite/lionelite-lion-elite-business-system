# Annual Tax Optimization

Run this with the CPA before year-end.

## Trust-level review
- Confirm grantor vs non-grantor treatment has not changed.
- Estimate trust ordinary income, capital gains, deductions, and tax due.
- If non-grantor, compare retaining income vs distributing authorized DNI to beneficiaries.
- Review estimated-tax payments.
- Review state residency/nexus rules affecting trust taxation.
- Confirm fiduciary accounting records and Form 1041/K-1 obligations.

## Entity-level review
- Review each LLC's tax classification.
- Compare S-corp election economics for profitable active businesses where trust ownership is eligible.
- Review reasonable compensation and payroll if an S corporation exists.
- Review retirement-plan options available to operating businesses.
- Review accountable-plan/reimbursement arrangements where legally available.
- Verify related-party payments are documented and commercially supportable.

## Investment / asset review
- Identify high-appreciation assets that may be better transferred before further growth.
- Harvest capital losses/gains when economically appropriate.
- Review depreciation schedules and cost-segregation opportunities for qualifying real estate.
- Flag potential 1031 transactions before a property sale closes.
- Review charitable planning before selling highly appreciated property.
