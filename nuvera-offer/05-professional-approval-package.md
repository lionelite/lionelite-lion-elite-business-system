# 05 — CPA / Attorney Approval Package

Nuvera turns the strategy into a clean review packet so the client's professionals can approve, reject, or modify each recommendation quickly.

## Package contents
- Executive summary
- Current-state ownership diagram
- Target-state ownership diagram
- Trust classification summary
- Entity list and tax elections
- Asset transfer matrix
- Distribution strategy questions
- Real-estate tax opportunities
- IP ownership recommendations
- Exit-planning flags
- Implementation sequence

## Approval table
| Recommendation | Why | Tax impact | Legal impact | CPA | Attorney | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Example: transfer holding-company interest to trust | Move future appreciation where appropriate | Review gift/basis/income-tax consequences | Review trust powers and transfer restrictions | Pending | Pending | HOLD |

## Rule
No recommendation requiring legal drafting, tax elections, valuation, gift-tax reporting, trust interpretation, or professional judgment moves to implementation until the applicable professional has approved it.
