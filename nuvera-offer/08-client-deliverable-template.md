# 08 — Client Deliverable Template

# Nuvera Optimization Blueprint

## Executive summary
What the client owns today, what is inefficient or exposed, and the highest-value changes Nuvera recommends.

## 1. Current structure
- Trusts
- Holding companies
- Operating entities
- Real estate
- IP
- Investments

## 2. Target structure
Show the recommended ownership diagram and explain the purpose of each entity.

## 3. Highest-value opportunities
Rank each recommendation by:
- Estimated impact: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW
- Urgency: NOW / THIS YEAR / FUTURE
- Complexity: SIMPLE / MODERATE / ADVANCED
- Approval required: CPA / ATTORNEY / BOTH / NONE

## 4. Tax optimization
Document only strategies relevant to the client, including:
- Trust income-tax treatment
- Business tax election opportunities
- Beneficiary distributions
- Real-estate depreciation / 1031 / cost-segregation review
- Appreciating-asset transfer opportunities
- IP ownership
- Exit planning

## 5. Asset-protection / separation map
Identify where valuable assets and operating liabilities are currently mixed and what separation is recommended.

## 6. Implementation plan
List every action in order, including who owns it and what must happen first.

## 7. Professional review
Provide the exact questions and recommendations that need CPA and attorney approval.

## 8. 12-month optimization calendar
Show annual filing, review, distribution, valuation, compensation, and planning checkpoints.

## Nuvera standard
The final client deliverable should be concise enough to understand in one sitting but detailed enough that a CPA, attorney, bookkeeper, or financial professional can act from it without rebuilding the client's entire financial picture from scratch.
