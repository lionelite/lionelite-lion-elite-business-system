# 02 — Current-State Map

Build one visual/documented map before recommending changes.

## Ownership hierarchy
```text
Individual / Family
        │
        ├── Trust(s)
        │     └── Assets / Entity Interests
        │
        ├── Holding Company
        │     ├── Operating LLCs
        │     ├── Real Estate LLCs
        │     └── IP / Investment Entities
        │
        └── Personally Owned Assets
```

## Required output
For every entity or major asset record:
- Legal owner
- Beneficial owner
- Taxpayer / EIN relationship
- Tax classification
- State of formation / situs
- Revenue or income source
- Major deductions
- Liability exposure
- Appreciating value potential
- Whether ownership is intentional or accidental

## Red-flag scan
- Valuable assets inside high-liability operating company
- Real estate mixed with operating business
- IP owned by operating company without reason
- Trust exists but owns little or nothing
- Entity tax election not aligned with economics
- Personal and business expenses mixed
- Multiple entities with no clear purpose
- Appreciating assets still personally owned despite estate-planning goal
- Trust income retained without annual tax comparison
- Planned sale before tax / estate strategy has been reviewed
