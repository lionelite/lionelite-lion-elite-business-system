# 03 — Tax Opportunity Engine

Nuvera reviews each client across the same opportunity categories and documents what is potentially useful, what is not applicable, and what requires professional approval.

## A. Trust optimization
- Confirm grantor vs non-grantor treatment
- Identify whether trust ownership supports estate-planning goals
- Compare trust-retained income vs beneficiary distributions when applicable
- Review whether grantor-paid tax can preserve trust assets
- Identify assets that may be better transferred before major appreciation

## B. Business tax structure
- Review current entity classification
- Model whether S-corp taxation may be beneficial for profitable active businesses
- Confirm trust eligibility before any S-corp ownership/election
- Keep separate businesses in separate entities where appropriate
- Separate high-liability operations from valuable passive assets

## C. Real estate
- Confirm ownership entity
- Track basis and depreciation
- Capture legitimate operating expenses and interest
- Flag cost-segregation review for qualifying properties
- Flag 1031 planning before qualifying investment-property sales
- Separate properties where liability or ownership economics justify it

## D. Intellectual property
- Identify trademarks, domains, digital products, software, brands, and licensing rights
- Determine whether valuable IP should be separated from operating liability
- Document ownership and licensing relationships
- Track IP basis, income, and potential appreciation

## E. Appreciating assets / estate planning
- Identify assets likely to grow materially
- Flag early-transfer opportunities for attorney/CPA review
- Track valuations and transfer dates
- Compare estate inclusion vs trust ownership consequences

## F. Distribution planning
- For non-grantor trusts, compare retaining taxable income vs distributing DNI to beneficiaries
- Track beneficiary brackets where legally relevant
- Never assume a distribution saves tax without modeling the character of the income

## G. Exit / liquidity planning
Before any major sale:
- Determine seller entity / owner
- Estimate gain and basis
- Review charitable planning where genuinely desired
- Review installment / rollover / reinvestment alternatives if applicable
- Confirm trust and estate consequences before signing a binding deal

## Decision output
Every opportunity receives one status:
- USE NOW
- MODEL FIRST
- PROFESSIONAL APPROVAL REQUIRED
- FUTURE OPPORTUNITY
- NOT APPLICABLE
