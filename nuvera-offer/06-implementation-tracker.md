## Status options
- NOT STARTED
- WAITING ON CLIENT
- WAITING ON CPA
- WAITING ON ATTORNEY
- READY TO EXECUTE
- COMPLETE
- BLOCKED

## Completion standard
An action is not complete until evidence exists: filed document, signed agreement, tax confirmation, ownership record, bank record, valuation, or professional written approval as applicable.
