# 07 — Annual Nuvera Review

Nuvera is not a one-time structure. Each client gets an annual optimization review so tax, ownership, estate, and entity planning stay aligned as income and assets change.

## Annual review checklist
- Update business revenue / profit
- Update entity ownership
- Update trust ownership and beneficiaries
- Update asset values
- Review new businesses / properties / IP
- Review retained trust income vs distributions where applicable
- Review owner compensation / S-corp economics where applicable
- Review depreciation and real-estate strategy
- Review planned asset sales or liquidity events
- Review new appreciated assets for transfer planning
- Review insurance and liability separation
- Review bookkeeping / bank-account separation
- Review filing deadlines and compliance
- Confirm attorney / CPA follow-ups completed

## Annual output
1. Updated ownership map
2. Updated asset map
3. Tax-opportunity summary
4. Action list ranked by impact
5. Professional approval items
6. Implementation tracker for the next 12 months
