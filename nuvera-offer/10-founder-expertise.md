# 10 — Founder Expertise & Credibility

## Alexander Ringfield — Founder-led expertise
Nuvera is built from direct experience helping clients understand and coordinate the full financial picture around retirement assets, business ownership, real estate, trusts, and tax-advantaged structuring.

## Self-Directed IRA Experience
Through professional experience at Equity Trust, Alexander has worked with clients exploring how self-directed retirement accounts can fit into broader investment and wealth strategies.

Core areas of practical experience include:
- Helping clients understand how Traditional IRA, Roth IRA, Solo 401(k), and self-directed IRA structures can be used for alternative investments
- Guiding clients through retirement-account investment structures involving real estate, private investments, LLCs, syndications, and other alternative assets
- Helping clients visualize how retirement accounts fit alongside personally owned businesses, real estate, and other investments
- Explaining account structure, funding paths, rollovers/transfers, checkbook-control structures, and investment workflows
- Identifying where retirement assets may create additional tax-advantaged growth opportunities within the client's broader financial picture

The process is designed to map:
- retirement assets
- personally owned assets
- business interests
- real estate
- trusts
- LLCs
- income sources

## Depreciation & Personally Owned Assets
Nuvera also incorporates knowledge of how depreciation and asset ownership can affect the financial picture, particularly for personally owned or investment assets.

Areas reviewed can include:
- real-estate depreciation
- cost-basis tracking
- business-use assets
- vehicle and equipment ownership
- improvements and capital expenditures
- entity ownership versus personal ownership
- depreciation recapture considerations
- timing of purchases and dispositions

The objective is to identify legitimate tax-planning opportunities and ownership inefficiencies before the client meets with their CPA.

## Nuvera's Expert Positioning
Nuvera's expertise is the ability to connect multiple areas that are often handled separately:

**IRA strategy + trust structure + business entities + real estate + depreciation + asset ownership + tax coordination.**

That integrated perspective is the foundation of the Nuvera offer.

## Professional Boundary
Nuvera provides financial-structure strategy, education, organization, modeling, and implementation coordination. It does not provide legal opinions, prepare tax returns, or replace the client's CPA, attorney, financial adviser, or licensed tax professional. Nuvera's role is to create the complete financial picture, identify opportunities and structural gaps, and bring those items to the appropriate professionals for final review and implementation.
