# 09 — Offer Positioning

## What Nuvera sells
Nuvera sells a coordinated wealth-structure optimization process for business owners, investors, and families with growing assets.

The client is not buying "a trust" or "an LLC." They are buying a complete architecture showing how their trust, businesses, real estate, IP, taxes, and future asset growth should work together.

## Core outcome
At the end of the engagement the client should know:
- what they own
- how each asset is owned
- which entities should exist
- what each entity is for
- where tax opportunities exist
- what should move into or under the trust
- what should remain separate
- what requires CPA approval
- what requires attorney approval
- what actions happen next and in what order

## Ideal client profile
- Business owner with growing profits
- Investor with multiple entities or properties
- Family using or considering an irrevocable trust
- Founder with valuable or appreciating company equity
- Owner with valuable trademarks, software, digital assets, or other IP
- Client anticipating a business or real-estate sale
- High-income client whose current structure grew organically and is now fragmented

## Nuvera differentiator
Most professionals work on one piece at a time. Nuvera coordinates the full picture and turns it into one implementation roadmap that the client's professionals can review and execute.

## Service boundary
Nuvera does not replace legal counsel, a CPA, or tax-return preparation. Nuvera identifies, organizes, models, documents, coordinates, and tracks the strategy so specialist professionals can implement the portions that require licensed judgment.
