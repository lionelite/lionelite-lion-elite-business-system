# 09 — Offer Positioning

## What Nuvera sells
Nuvera sells a coordinated wealth-structure optimization process for business owners, investors, and families with growing assets.

The client is not buying "a trust" or "an LLC." They are buying a complete architecture showing how their retirement assets, trust, businesses, real estate, IP, taxes, depreciation opportunities, and future asset growth should work together.

## Core outcome
At the end of the engagement the client should know:
- what they own
- how each asset is owned
- how retirement accounts fit into the overall financial picture
- which entities should exist
- what each entity is for
- where tax opportunities exist
- where depreciation may create planning opportunities
- what should move into or under the trust
- what should remain separate
- what requires CPA approval
- what requires attorney approval
- what actions happen next and in what order

## Founder-led expertise
Nuvera is built from Alexander Ringfield's experience helping clients understand self-directed retirement-account strategies and create a broader financial picture that connects retirement assets with real estate, private investments, business interests, LLCs, and long-term wealth planning.

The Nuvera framework combines working knowledge across:
- Self-Directed IRAs and alternative investments
- Traditional and Roth retirement structures
- Solo 401(k) and rollover/transfer workflows
- Checkbook-control and investment LLC structures
- Trust and beneficiary planning concepts
- Business and holding-company architecture
- Personally owned and investment real estate
- Depreciation and asset-ownership considerations
- Pre-sale and long-term wealth planning

This integrated perspective is central to the Nuvera offer: connect the pieces first, then coordinate the appropriate CPA, attorney, and other licensed professionals for implementation.

## Ideal client profile
- Business owner with growing profits
- Investor with multiple entities or properties
- Family using or considering an irrevocable trust
- Founder with valuable or appreciating company equity
- Owner with valuable trademarks, software, digital assets, or other IP
- Investor with significant retirement assets seeking alternative-investment flexibility
- Client anticipating a business or real-estate sale
- High-income client whose current structure grew organically and is now fragmented

## Nuvera differentiator
Most professionals work on one piece at a time. Nuvera coordinates the full picture across retirement assets, trusts, entities, real estate, depreciation, IP, and tax strategy, then turns it into one implementation roadmap that the client's professionals can review and execute.

## Service boundary
Nuvera does not replace legal counsel, a CPA, tax-return preparation, or licensed investment advice. Nuvera identifies, organizes, models, documents, coordinates, and tracks the strategy so specialist professionals can implement the portions that require licensed judgment.
