# 04 — Target Structure

The target structure is built from the client's goals, risk profile, asset mix, and professional review. Nuvera does not force every client into the same entity stack.

## Common architecture
```text
Irrevocable Trust / Estate-Planning Trust
                 │
                 ▼
          Holding Company
        ┌────────┼────────┐
        ▼        ▼        ▼
 Operating LLC  IP LLC  Investment / RE LLCs
```

## Design rules
- Trust owns strategic interests where appropriate.
- Operating companies handle active business activity.
- Real estate is not casually mixed with unrelated operating liabilities.
- Valuable IP can be separated where protection/licensing benefits justify it.
- Each entity has a documented purpose.
- Each tax election is chosen separately based on the economics and eligibility.
- Bank accounts, books, contracts, and ownership records must match the legal structure.

## Deliverable
For each client, produce:
1. Current-state diagram
2. Target-state diagram
3. Entity-by-entity purpose statement
4. Ownership percentages
5. Proposed tax classification/election review
6. Asset-transfer list
7. Required professional approvals
8. Implementation sequence
